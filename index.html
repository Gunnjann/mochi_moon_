02 Our story

A tiny dumpling cart, a very big moon.

Mochi Moon began in 2015 as a cart outside a Bengaluru metro station — one bamboo steamer, a borrowed stove and Chef Rin's grandmother's rule: eighteen folds, never fewer.

Today we're a forty-seat dining room with the same promise. Everything is made by hand, every morning, in small batches.
